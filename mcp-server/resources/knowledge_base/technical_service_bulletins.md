# Technical Service Bulletins (TSB) -- Active Index

*Condensed internal digest of manufacturer TSBs relevant to parts Torque Tune
stocks. Service writers use this to tell customers *why* a part is being
recommended and whether it's a known, documented issue rather than a
one-off diagnosis.*

## TSB-2021-045: Premature Brake Pad Wear, Compact SUV Platform

- Affects vehicles with the CS-3 brake caliper assembly (category `brakes`,
  supplier prefix `TPD-`).
- Root cause: caliper slide pins under-lubricated at factory assembly.
- Fix: replace pads **and** slide pin kit together, never pads alone --
  replacing pads alone results in repeat comeback within 3,000 miles.
- Not a warranty defect under WT-100; this is a maintenance-interval issue,
  billed to the customer.

## TSB-2022-118: Alternator Whine at Idle, V6 Platform

- Affects alternators with supplier SKU pattern `CAE-ALT-*` manufactured
  before week 22 of 2022.
- Symptom: high-pitched whine at idle, disappears above 1,500 RPM.
- Fix: replace with current-production alternator (post week-22 stock).
  Do **not** attempt a bearing-only repair; the bearing is not
  serviceable separately on this unit.
- This is a warranty-eligible defect under WT-204 if the unit is within
  the 24-month window and was installed by a certified technician.

## TSB-2023-072: CV Joint Boot Cracking, All-Wheel-Drive Models

- Affects CV joints with supplier prefix `IDS-` and manufacture date before
  Q1 2023.
- Symptom: grease leakage visible at the boot seam within the first
  10,000 miles.
- Fix: replace the entire CV joint assembly, not just the boot --
  boot-only replacement does not address the underlying seam defect and
  will fail again.
- Remanufactured units (`-RM` suffix) are **not** covered by this bulletin;
  the defect is specific to first-run production units.

## TSB-2024-009: Cabin Air Filter Housing Clip Fragility

- Affects housings paired with Meridian Filtration Co. filters
  (`MFC-` prefix) on the sedan platform.
- Symptom: retaining clip snaps during filter replacement if the housing
  is more than 3 years old.
- Fix: inspect the clip before ordering a replacement filter; if the clip
  is original-to-vehicle and more than 3 years old, order the housing
  clip kit alongside the filter, not the filter alone.

## TSB-2024-118: Clutch Slave Cylinder Cross-Contamination

- Affects clutch assemblies from Ironclad Drivetrain Supply (`IDS-`
  prefix) paired with a specific aftermarket slave cylinder.
- Symptom: soft clutch pedal within 2,000 miles of install, caused by
  incompatible seal material reacting with certain brake fluid types.
- Fix: only pair IDS clutch kits with OEM-spec slave cylinders; flag any
  work order that lists an aftermarket slave cylinder alongside an IDS
  clutch kit for a second inspection before closing.
- This is a compatibility issue, not a manufacturing defect -- not
  warranty-eligible under WT-441 on its own, but the clutch kit itself
  remains covered if the seal contamination is later ruled out.
